About the blog

Patterns in Code and Culture is where I document how my thinking evolves.

This Blog is a personal journal in public--a space to track what I learn in tech, AI, society, psychology, and agriculture while growing from student to systems thinker.

Who I am

A learner, builder, and observer trying to connect systems with lived reality.

I am documenting my path from student to engineer, and hopefully toward AI research and systems thinking. I'm interested in how technology shapes people, how people shape technology, and how those ideas might also connect to agriculture and smart farming.

Why I'm writing

A working memory for ideas, mistakes, and shifts in perspective.

I want this space to help me remember what I learn, what I question, and how my mindset changes over time. Some posts will be rough notes, some will be essays, and some will simply capture observations I do not want to lose.

The four pillars

The themes this blog will keep returning to.

These pillars help me organize my writing so the blog stays personal, interdisciplinary, and grounded in the questions I actually care about.

From student to systems

Notes from my journey in Tech/IT, AI, tools, research, and the gradual development of a systems mindset.

02

From fields to models

Reflection on agriculture, startup realities, and the future connection between farming, data, and intelligent systems.

03

Beyond the herd

Observations on society, psychology, human behavior, and the common misconceptions people carry about technology and AI.

04

Working memory

A place for reflective notes, evolving opinions, raw thoughts, and lessons I want to preserve as I grow.

Closing note

This blog is meant to grow in public, not appear fully formed.

I am starting with clarity of intention, not perfection of execution. The writing, structure, and design will evolve with time, just like the person behind them.
