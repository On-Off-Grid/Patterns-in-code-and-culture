Why this exists

A space to think in public without losing the private thread.

I want this blog to hold together several parts of my life that usually live in separate rooms: technical work, self-awareness, social observation, and the realities of agriculture in my country. Some posts will be short notes. Others will be essays, build logs, or uncomfortable questions.

Starting point

This will begin with notes, not perfection.

Early entries may look like lab notes, short reflections, or rough observations. That is intentional. I want the structure to grow with me instead of pretending I already finished the journey.
